## SRC-TURNER-2022 — Important Milestone Reached on Seattle Aquarium Ocean Pavilion

- Publisher: Turner Construction Company
- Published: 2022-11-21
- Source type: general_contractor_project_update
- Evidence class: Contractor-reported construction facts
- Source: https://www.turnerconstruction.com/insights/important-milestone-reached-on-seattle-aquarium-ocean-pavilion
- Location: Article body, concrete tank pour and formwork description
- Limit: Project update, not an issued-for-construction drawing or contract specification. Preserve Turner's wording and units when answering.

### TURNER-TANK-QUANTITIES — main tank reported quantities

Turner Construction reports that the Ocean Pavilion main tank wall is at least 2 feet thick and that the tank pour used 680 cubic yards of concrete and 355 tons of reinforcing steel. Turner describes the reinforcement amount as about four times that of a typical core.

### TURNER-FORMWORK — curved tank formwork

Turner reports that the non-straight tank geometry required 229 foam formwork panels. The panels were CNC cut, attached to PERI backing, and coated with truck-bed liner. Rhino and Grasshopper were used to work with the tank's double-curved concrete geometry.

## SRC-TERRACON-2024 — Concrete, steel testing helps bring Seattle Aquarium to life

- Publisher: Terracon
- Published: 2024-12-11
- Source type: special_inspection_provider_case_study
- Evidence class: Inspection provider's retrospective case report
- Source: https://www.terracon.com/2024/12/11/case-study-seattle-aquarium-ocean-pavilion/
- Location: Challenge, Solution, and Results sections
- Limit: Terracon reports project facts and testing work; the article is not the approved mix design or contract specification. It calls the 10,000 psi material grout, not concrete.

### TERRACON-MATERIAL-SELECTION — reported tank material and placement challenge

Terracon describes a custom-built 325,000-gallon tank. It reports nearly 3 million pounds of reinforcing steel in the walls and says standard concrete was unsuitable because aggregate could separate before settling in the congested reinforcement. A high-strength 10,000 psi grout was chosen instead.

### TERRACON-TESTING — special inspection and material testing

Terracon reports that inspectors examined reinforcement and grout in sections as the tank was built, completed nearly 650 material tests, and performed field and laboratory testing that included concrete, shotcrete, and grout.

## SRC-MKA-PROJECT — Seattle Aquarium Ocean Pavilion

- Publisher: Magnusson Klemencic Associates
- Published: Date not listed
- Source type: structural_engineer_project_page
- Evidence class: Project engineer's design description
- Source: https://www.mka.com/projects/seattle-aquarium-ocean-pavilion/
- Location: Project description
- Limit: Provides structural context, not design calculations, approved dimensions, or material acceptance requirements.

### MKA-STRUCTURAL-ROLE — tank structural role and design context

MKA describes the geometrically complex, cantilevered concrete tank as part of the building's primary gravity- and lateral-force-resisting systems. The page says the tank's unusual geometry and sloshing water effects affected seismic design and detailing.

### MKA-FORMWORK-CONTEXT — custom formwork context

MKA says the tank design incorporated 3D printing and computer numerical control for custom-cut formwork.
