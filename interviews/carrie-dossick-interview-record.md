# Edited Interview Record: Dr. Carrie Dossick

Interviewee: Dr. Carrie Dossick, UW School of Construction Management.

Project context: AI-assisted document retrieval for concrete subcontractors preparing RFIs, using the Seattle Aquarium Ocean Pavilion as the project lens.

Source: [Team document: Interview Questions + Response](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1mWZ5glFVbaz-HXh6wqxvimRZcSYoCS94IFlVUTWIhmA/edit?tab=t.t5zpa05x5orj). The [original team notes](carrie-dossick-notes.md) are preserved separately.

Format: This record is organized by topic and paraphrased from the team's interview notes. It is not a recording-based transcript. No speaker dialogue has been reconstructed.

## Professional background

The notes describe Dr. Dossick's experience in engineering and construction research, including ethnographic research on how people use technology. Her work has examined the emergence of 3D modeling and databases in the industry. The notes also connect her research to pricing and the effects of information on collaboration.

## AI use in construction

According to the meeting summary, Dr. Dossick described LLMs and other new technologies as still being tested in construction. Companies remain cautious about adopting AI in some information workflows because construction projects involve high stakes.

## People and information presentation

The notes attribute to Dr. Dossick the view that people drive change through their use of technology. They describe her observations of people using 3D technologies and suggest that less abstract information can support clearer discussion.

She emphasized that the agent should remain focused on documents. AI suggestions should stay separate from informed human decisions.

## Feedback on the prototype

The meeting summary reports that Dr. Dossick saw potential in the team's approach and considered accurate document retrieval a relevant bottleneck. It records the following suggestions for the prototype:

- Allow users to open older versions of documents.
- Provide a document change log.
- Expand the missing-information feature.

## Related tools

The notes record that Dr. Dossick mentioned Willow, Procore, Autodesk and Bluebeam when asked about existing tools with similar functions.

## Team follow-up recorded in the notes

The team planned to consider how retrieved information should be displayed and what explanations users would need. It also planned to examine the tools mentioned during the interview to understand stakeholder needs and identify possible improvements to the website. These are the team's follow-up plans, separate from the interviewee's comments.
