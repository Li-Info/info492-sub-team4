STAKEHOLDER MEETING:

Dr. Carrie Dossick from the UW School of Construction Management was willing to sit down with us for an interview and act as a potential stakeholder in the Ocean Pavilion Project. Dr. Dossick has experience both in the industry and in construction research. Her studies combine traditional construction management research techniques with ethnographic research; studying how building information management systems impact human interaction. She was able to give us valuable insight into how AI agents and LLMs have been integrated into the construction workflow. LLMs, like other new technologies, are still being thoroughly experimented with in the industry. Most companies are still hesitant to fully adopt AI into certain information flows because of the high stakes associated with construction projects. In relation to our project, we will take this information into careful consideration when deciding how to display information retrieved by the AI agent and what disclaimers to add for our users. Dr. Dossick sees potential in our solution and agrees that accurate document retrieval is a highly relevant bottleneck to address. Some suggestions she had for improving our current prototype include: a) adding the option to pull up older versions of certain documents, b) adding the ability to see a change log for documents, and c) adding more capabilities to our “missing information” feature. She emphasized that our AI agent should remain document-oriented, maintaining the separation between AI suggestions and informed human decisions.

When asked about possible existing resources with the same or similar functions, Dr. Dossick recommended that we look into tools such as Willow (affiliated with Microsoft), Procore, Autodesk, and Bluebeam. In the next project portion, we will be sure to investigate these resources to better understand the needs of stakeholders in the construction industry. Examining these tools will also give us ideas for what improvements can be made to our webpage.
